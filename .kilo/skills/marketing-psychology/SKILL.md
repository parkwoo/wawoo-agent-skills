# marketing-psychology

**One line**: tighten **how** you show price, plans, and CTAs — do not invent new claims.

## Read first

- Principles and steps: [`references/framing-principles.md`](references/framing-principles.md)
- Workflow map (JA): `docs/COPY-MARKETING-GUIDE.ja.md` §3

## Quick procedure

1. Audience + single decision (e.g. “pick a plan”, “start trial”).
2. Choose **2–3** principles from the reference table.
3. Deliver **before / after** snippets for the same facts.
4. List **misleading risks**; human approves before publish.

## Not in scope

- Market sizing (`/market-plan` + `research`)
- Title/hook craft (`viral-hooks`)
- Full article draft (`/writing`)
