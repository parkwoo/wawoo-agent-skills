# Framing principles (actionable)

Use **at most three** principles per piece. Always pair with honest copy (no fabricated scarcity or numbers).

## When to invoke this skill

- Pricing page or plan comparison layout
- CTA label and button order
- Benefit bullets vs feature lists
- Onboarding or email subject lines (not full fraud review)

## Steps (agent)

1. **Audience + job**: one sentence — who and what they need to decide now.
2. **Pick 2–3 principles** from the table below (say which you chose and why).
3. **Before / after**: show the same fact with old framing vs proposed framing (≤120 words each).
4. **Risk check**: list what could mislead; downgrade or remove if unsure.

## Principle menu

| id | Use when | Do | Don't |
|----|----------|-----|--------|
| `social-proof` | New or anxious buyers | Real counts, logos, quotes with source | Fake “10,000+ users” |
| `anchoring` | Multiple plans | Show context price or “full” tier first | Hide true cheapest path |
| `loss-aversion` | Sticky behavior | State what they keep missing (true) | Fear without remedy |
| `specificity` | Weak generic copy | Concrete outcome + timeframe | Vague “boost productivity” |
| `cognitive-ease` | Dense pages | One primary CTA, short lines | Ten equal buttons |
| `reciprocity` | Free tier / lead magnet | Give value first, then ask | Bait without delivery |
